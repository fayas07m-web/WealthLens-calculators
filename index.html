How SIP Works

You invest a fixed amount regularly. Over time, your investment can compound, meaning returns can generate additional returns. Longer investment periods can have a significant effect on potential wealth.

What is Inflation?

Inflation reduces the purchasing power of money over time. The inflation adjustment estimates what your future amount may be worth in today's purchasing power.
